STAGE 1 / 4 • PLATFORM OVERVIEW

What is NEXUS-SHIELD & What Problem Does It Solve?

Enterprise perimeter firewalls and basic antivirus are blind to lateral movement. Once an attacker breaches a single workstation, they quietly traverse internal protocols (SMB, RDP, WMI) toward your most critical databases.

🌐

Dynamic Network Graph $G = (V, E)$

NEXUS-SHIELD maintains a real-time, in-memory topological graph. Nodes are verified endpoints; edges are live protocol communication flows with directional weights.

🧠

Behavioral Anomaly Baselines

Profiles normal peer-to-peer traffic patterns. Any unusual workstation-to-workstation administrative connection (e.g. PC to PC over port 445) immediately flags an alarm.

🎯

Blast Radius & Attack Paths

Calculates the shortest graph paths from any suspicious machine to high-value crown jewels (Domain Controllers, SQL databases), scoring composite risk from 0 to 100.

⚡

Sub-Second Automated Quarantine

When threat scores exceed threshold (85+), the containment engine automatically issues native Windows Firewall rules, instantly severing malicious sockets.

STAGE 2 / 4 • HOW IT WORKS

The 5 Core Autonomous Defense Engines

Built from the ground up with a modular architecture that turns raw packet streams into actionable, automated containment.

01

Packet Sniffing & Flow Parser

Asynchronous capture captures raw frames and decodes Ethernet, ARP, IPv4, TCP, UDP, and ICMP, maintaining bidirectional state tables.

core/capture.py • core/flow_tracker.py
02

Continuous Asset Discovery

Passively and actively fingerprints IP, MAC manufacturer, hostname, OS type, and open listening ports, categorizing asset criticality.

discovery/asset_inventory.py
03

Multi-Vector Threat Detectors

Detects horizontal port sweeps, stealth vertical scans, and unauthorized peer-to-peer administrative pivots (SMB 445, RDP 3389).

detector/port_scan.py • detector/lateral_movement.py
04

In-Memory Attack Path Graph

NetworkX directed graph evaluates shortest paths to sensitive servers and quantifies total blast radius around compromised nodes.

graph/network_graph.py • engine/risk_scorer.py
05

Automated Windows Firewall Quarantine

Generates and applies native netsh advfirewall rules to instantly block outbound network traffic from infected machines.

containment/quarantine.py
