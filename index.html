How NEXUS-SHIELD Works

Designed & Developed by Amarthaluri Srinivasu
1

Listens in Real-Time

Monitors network packets continuously (L2 through L7) and keeps track of who is talking to whom in an in-memory graph $G = (V, E)$.

2

Spots Hidden Lateral Movement

Traditional firewalls watch the outside internet. NEXUS-SHIELD watches the inside. If a marketing workstation suddenly scans internal servers or talks to another PC over SMB (Port 445), it catches it immediately.

3

Blocks Instantly via Firewall

When threat risk exceeds 85/100, it doesn't just send an email—it instantly issues a native Windows Firewall rule to isolate the infected machine in 0.4 milliseconds.
